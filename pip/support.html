Frequently Asked Questions

Does Pip Coloring work without Wi-Fi?

Yes. Once installed, every page works 100% offline. A connection is only needed to buy or restore a subscription.

How does my child color a page?

Pick a page, choose a color, then tap a shape with the Fill tool to color it in one tap. The Crayon tool draws freely, the Eraser removes color, and Undo goes back a step. Tap Done to watch the page come alive.

What is free, and what does the subscription unlock?

The fox page is free. A subscription unlocks all 50 pages. You can choose Weekly (with a 3-day free trial for new subscribers) or Yearly. Prices are shown in the app before purchase.

How do I restore my subscription on a new iPhone?

Open the settings (gear icon on the home screen), answer the grown-up question, then tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple ID that was used to subscribe.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Pip Coloring. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period to avoid renewal.

Is it safe for my child?

Pip Coloring has no ads, no tracking, no analytics and no accounts. Purchases, settings and external links are protected by a parental gate. See our Privacy Policy for details.
