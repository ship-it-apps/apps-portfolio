Frequently Asked Questions

How does Mijo plan my week?

Tell Mijo who eats at home, the foods to avoid, how many meat or fish nights you want, the evenings to cook and your favorite cuisines. Mijo picks one easy dinner (15 to 25 minutes) for each chosen evening and builds the grocery list.

Can I change a dish?

Yes. Tap the swap icon next to any dinner to choose one of three ideas, or roll the dice. Tap “Other dishes” on the home screen for a whole new week.

How do I change my household or excluded foods?

Open the family icon on the home screen, then “At the table” or “Excluded foods”. Quantities and the grocery list update straight away, and any dish that no longer fits is replaced.

What does the subscription include?

The subscription unlocks the app: the weekly dinners, swaps, the grocery list, the cooking mode and reminders. Choose Yearly or Weekly; a free trial may be offered to new subscribers. Prices and the trial length are shown in the app before purchase.

How do I restore or cancel my subscription?

To restore, open Settings in Mijo and tap “Restore purchases” with the same Apple ID. To cancel, open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Mijo, at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.

What happens to my data?

Mijo has no account, no ads and no tracking. Your household, excluded foods and menus stay on your iPhone. See our Privacy Policy for details.
