Weekly family meal plan: plan every dinner in 30 seconds

“What’s for dinner tonight?” Every evening, the same question. Planning the whole week at once, grocery list included, solves it before it starts.

In short

  • Planning dinners once a week removes the daily question and last-minute shopping trips.
  • The key: simple dishes (15 to 25 minutes), sized for your household and free of excluded foods.
  • A single list sorted by aisle gathers every ingredient for the week.

Why plan once a week

Deciding every evening stacks the day’s fatigue on top of whatever is in the fridge. Planning on Sunday lets you choose calmly, balance the week and shop only once.

What matters is not the perfect recipe: it is knowing, at 5:30 pm, what you are cooking tonight.

The four-step method

1. Count who eats at home (a child counts as half a portion). 2. Pick the evenings you want a dinner planned. 3. List the foods to exclude. 4. Spread meat, fish and veggie nights across the week.

Then add up the ingredients and sort them by aisle: fruit and veg, chilled, pantry, frozen. That is exactly what Mijo does, in 30 seconds.

Frequently asked questions

How many dinners should I plan per week?

Only the evenings you cook at home: Mijo lets you choose which ones.

How do I handle a picky child?

Exclude the foods in question: they will not appear in the plan or in swaps.

Can I share the grocery list?

Yes, the list can be shared in one tap.
