Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. No sign-up, no ads, no trackers: your preferences stay on your iPhone.

Does Mijo handle dietary needs?

Yes. Excluded foods (pork, beef, fish, gluten, lactose, peanuts…) never appear, neither in the plan nor in swaps.

How long do the recipes take?

Dishes are simple and take 15 to 25 minutes.

How does the subscription work?

Mijo uses an auto-renewing yearly or weekly subscription. Any free trial length and the price are shown before you buy.
