Frequently asked questions

How does First work?

The apps you choose stay locked with Screen Time until you read the verse of the day: about 30 seconds with a verse, a reflection, a prayer and an optional quiz. Then they unlock.

Can First see my apps?

No. Apple only gives First opaque tokens: First cannot see the names of your apps or how you use them.

Do I need an account?

No. Everything is stored on your iPhone, with no iCloud and no First server.

What if it’s an emergency?

A 15-minute emergency unlock is available at any time.

How much does First cost?

First Premium costs €39.99 per year with a 3-day free trial, or €6.99 per month. The subscription renews automatically and can be cancelled in your Apple ID settings.
