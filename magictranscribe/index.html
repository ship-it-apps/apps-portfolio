Features

Your voice notes, finally put to use.

Live transcription

Tap the red button and talk: the text appears as you speak, even offline, thanks to Apple’s on-device speech recognition.

Summary, key points and chapters

When you stop, Bibo titles the note, summarizes it, pulls out the key points and splits it into chapters, right on the device.

WhatsApp voice messages and voice memos

Frequently asked questions

Which languages can I transcribe?

English, French, German, Spanish, Italian and Portuguese. You pick the language for each recording.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Transcription happens on the iPhone with Apple’s speech recognition, once the language module is downloaded.

Where are my notes stored?

Only on your iPhone, with no Magic Transcribe account or server. You can exclude them from iCloud backup and lock the app with Face ID.

How do I transcribe a WhatsApp voice message?

In WhatsApp, tap Share on the voice message, then choose Magic Scribe. Bibo transcribes and summarizes it.

How does the subscription work?

Magic Transcribe requires the Magic Transcribe Pro subscription, monthly or yearly, renewing automatically. The price is shown in the app before purchase, and you can cancel in your Apple ID settings.
