Frequently asked questions

How does the app hear my guitar?

Riffo uses your iPhone’s microphone to recognise the notes and chords you play, in real time. Audio stays on your iPhone and is never recorded.

I have never played. Is it for me?

Yes. The path starts with holding the guitar and playing your first notes, then moves on one idea at a time.

How much does Riffo cost?

Riffo requires a Riffo Pro subscription: €3.99 per week or €39.99 per year, with a 7-day free trial for new subscribers on the yearly plan.

Which guitar do I need?

Acoustic, classical or electric, for right- and left-handed players. The app runs on iPhone with iOS 18.0 or later.
