Frequently asked questions

How does the app hear my guitar?

Guitar Scale Quest uses your iPhone’s microphone to detect the pitch of the notes you play, in real time.

Do I need to read sheet music?

No. Scales are shown on a guitar fretboard, with the next note highlighted.

How much does Guitar Scale Quest cost?

The app is free. ScaleQuest Pro costs €1.99 per week or €19.99 per year, with a 7-day free trial for new subscribers.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone, running iOS 18.0 or later.
