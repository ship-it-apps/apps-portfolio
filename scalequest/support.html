Frequently Asked Questions

How does Riffo hear my guitar?

Riffo uses your iPhone's built-in microphone to analyze the sound of your guitar in real time. Play the note or chord the lesson asks for and the app tells you right away if you got it. For best results, play in a quiet room and keep your phone close to your guitar. Audio is processed on your device and never recorded.

What will I learn?

A step-by-step path in 6 sections: foundations, first chords, rhythm and songs, the fretboard, the minor pentatonic, then major, blues and improvisation. Lessons are short, one idea at a time, with a challenge at the end of each section.

Does it work with acoustic and electric guitars?

Yes. Riffo works with acoustic, classical and electric guitars, for right- and left-handed players. With an electric guitar, play unplugged or at low volume near the microphone.

Why isn't the app detecting my notes correctly?

Make sure your guitar is in tune (Riffo has a built-in tuner), play in a quiet room and keep your iPhone within arm's reach. Background noise, other instruments or playing too softly can affect detection. Also check that microphone access is enabled in Settings > Riffo.

How do subscriptions work?

Riffo requires a Riffo Pro subscription, weekly or yearly. The yearly plan includes a 7-day free trial for new subscribers. You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time in Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions. If you change iPhone, tap Restore Purchases on the subscription screen.

Which languages are supported?

Riffo is available in English, French and Spanish. The app language follows your iPhone's language settings.
