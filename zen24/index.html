Frequently asked questions

Is Zen24 the official Beds24 app?

No. Zen24 is an independent app made by SHIP IT. It connects to your Beds24 account to display and manage your properties on iPhone.

How much does Zen24 cost?

Zen24 is free. Premium costs $6.99 per month, with a 7-day free trial.

Which devices does Zen24 support?

iPhone, running iOS 18.0 or later.

Which languages are available?

English, French, German, Spanish and Portuguese.

Can I export bookings to my calendar?

Yes. Zen24 exports your booking calendar as an ICS file that works with any calendar app.
